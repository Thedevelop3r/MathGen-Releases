# MathGen privacy policy

MathGen does not collect, store or send any personal data.

- It works entirely offline and never connects to the internet itself. (The
  links in Help → About open in your web browser, only when you click them.)
- It has no accounts, analytics, advertising or crash reporting.
- Your equations and settings stay on your computer: settings are saved in
  your Windows user profile (or next to `mathgen.exe` in portable mode), and
  equations only where you save, copy or export them.

Questions: open an issue at
<https://github.com/Thedevelop3r/MathGen-Releases/issues>.
